FAQ

What size TV do I need?

For a registration desk, a menu board, or a small meeting room, the 43" is plenty. For slides in a function room, go 65". If people are watching from the back of a conference room, take the 86" or talk to us about an LED screen instead.

Do the TVs come with stands?

Yes. Every TV comes with its stand included in the hire price. The 43" comes on a truss stand with a baseplate, and the 65" and 86" come on stands. There is nothing extra to hire to get it standing up.

Can you deliver and set up?

Yes. Pick up from our warehouse in Sydenham, or we will deliver the TV, set it up, test your content on it, and collect it after the event.
